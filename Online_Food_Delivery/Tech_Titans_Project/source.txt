------------------
Name: Online Food Dataset
Provider: Kaggle
Link: https: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/srisyra02/online-food-ordering-dataset
Size: 388 rows x 14 columns
Description: Contains demographic information (Age, Gender, Marital Status,
Occupation, Monthly Income, Educational Qualifications, Family size),
location information (latitude, longitude, Pin code), and order details
(Output, Feedback) collected from an online food ordering platform.
